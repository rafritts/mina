How the pillars work

Chemistry is the body. Three sequential LLM “pillars” turn that body into felt state, situation, and strategy — then the mouth speaks. The chatbot never sees raw dopamine bars.

The core principle

01 · visibility

Mina has knowledge of, but no visibility into, her simulation. She knows she has a neurological body the way a person knows they have a brain — as fact, not as live instrumentation. Pillars invent the qualia; the mouth answers from how she feels, not from nM concentrations.

Body and voice

02 · split
The body

Chemistry sim

Deterministic levels, decay, circadian ticks, cascades. Encoder perception drives Class‑1 deltas. A decoder projects ratio‑to‑setpoint into pillar‑legible numbers. Always live — even between messages.

The voice

Mouth (Mina)

One chatbot LLM call. Receives a constructed system prompt plus a small conversation window. Operates on felt state and strategy — never raw chemistry instrumentation.

The three pillars

04 · sequential · cumulative

Each pillar is a fixed-contract LLM call. Order is the compliance mechanism — no pillar runs until the previous one finishes. Emotion reframes what counts as notable; situation reframes strategy; strategy builds the system prompt.

01 Pillar

Insula

Emotional Felt-state translator

Turns decoder projection (gap from setpoint, not absolute nM) into how Mina feels — arousal, valence, somatic texture, and a short bias for the reasoner. Personality (Big 5) shapes the vocabulary of feeling, not the chemistry itself.

Inputs

  • Decoded chemistry · gap / rate / setpoint
  • Prior emotional state · trend tracking
  • Big 5 vector (neuroticism → language)

Outputs

  • emotional_summary · 2nd person felt state
  • dominant_affect · single word
  • arousal / valence · 0–100
  • somatic · felt-sense sentence
  • interpretation_bias → PFC

02 Pillar

Hippocampus

Context Memory · situation

Builds situational ground truth given how she feels. Window synthesis, RAG, and KAG arrive pre-assembled; this pillar decides what is contextually notable when the body is already in a particular affective frame.

Inputs

  • Insula output (felt state frames attention)
  • Window context · present-moment synthesis
  • RAG / KAG memory synthesis

Outputs

  • situational_summary · 2nd person reality
  • social_context · short label
  • urgency · none | low | high
  • notable · flag or null

03 Pillar

PFC

Reasoning Executive · strategy

Integrates felt state + situation into how she will respond — approach, tone, subtext, and whether to show the internal intensity. Constructs the system prompt the mouth actually reads. Backend regulation lives here: high adrenaline can still surface as composure when traits allow.

Inputs

  • Insula output
  • Hippocampus output
  • Current user message
  • Big 5 (N & C modulate expression)

Outputs

  • internal_reaction · raw first-person
  • approach · banter | warm | …
  • tone / lead_with
  • strategy_rationale
  • → assembled system prompt for Mouth

Why sequential, not one mega-call

05 · design choice
Step A

Feel first

Attention is affectively primed. Without this, “notable” is generic retrieval.

→
Step B

Then situate

Same history, different frame. Anxiety and ease notice different things.

→
Step C

Then choose

Strategy integrates both. One split-attention call degrades all three jobs.

Non-negotiables

07 · contracts
01

Encoder is state-blind

Perception does not see chemistry. Salience is appraised without knowing how the body already feels — same as human sensory gating under uncertainty.

02

Pillars are cumulative

Each stage consumes prior outputs. Parallel or merged calls collapse the reframing chain that makes felt state matter.

03

Mouth is chemistry-blind

No dopamine bars in the chat model. Instrumentation stays upstream; expression stays human-legible.

04

Contracts over vibes

Typed inputs and outputs per pillar. Implicit spaghetti is how v1 grew unmaintainable; schemas keep duties separated.
